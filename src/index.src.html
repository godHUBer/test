How this dealership works

Quality, transparency, and a
straight answer.

We are not a marketplace and we do not certify cars we have not looked at. What follows is the specification the dealership has actually published for each car — with the gaps left visible.

01 · Selection

Cars are taken in one at a time

Multi-brand, so the floor follows what is genuinely well kept rather than what one manufacturer wants moved. Ask what the team liked and disliked about any car — that question is usually more useful than a badge.

02 · Transparency

What the listing states, and what it does not

Year, variant, odometer, fuel and registration city are published for every car on this site. Where a listing is silent — a gearbox type, say — the page reads not stated rather than guessing on your behalf.

03 · Assistance

One contact, enquiry to handover

The same desk arranges the viewing, the paperwork, the exchange of your old car and the introductions for finance or insurance. Repairs and servicing stay with the same team afterwards.

04 · Viewing

See it in Kolkata before you decide

Photographs here are taken at the showroom, not in a studio. Book a slot, walk around it, start it cold, and drive it on roads you actually use.

answered honestly.

01How can I browse the cars you have?

The collection page holds {{PUBLISHED}} of the {{FLOOR}} cars the dealership keeps on its books — those are the ones published online with photographs and specifications. The balance is sold directly from the floor. Call {{PHONE}} and ask for the full list; it takes a minute and it is current in a way a website cannot always be.

02Can I schedule a showroom visit?

Yes — phone ahead, name the car, and agree a time. {{PH:hours}}. Several listings carry a “free home test drive” note, meaning the team will bring the car to you inside Kolkata; {{PH:testdrive}}.

03How do I enquire about a particular vehicle?

Each car page has an enquiry form that states the car, the year, the odometer and your contact details in one message, so nobody has to ask which Creta you mean. It composes an email on your device — nothing is stored on this website. Phone is faster if you want an answer the same day.

04Can I sell or exchange my existing car?

Yes. Used car exchange is one of the dealership’s published services, and the sell form asks for the same details the showroom’s own form collects. {{PH:valuation}}.

05What documentation do I get with a car?

This site publishes what the listing states: registration city, odometer, fuel type and, where the dealer flagged it, service history. Ownership papers, insurance status and the PUC certificate come from the dealership on request — ask before you pay a booking amount. {{PH:inspection}}.

06Do you help with financing?

{{PH:finance}}. The specification list on each car page is a useful starting point for what a lender will want from you, and the showroom desk will point you at the paperwork.
