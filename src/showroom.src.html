A visit, start to finish

Give yourself an hour.

  1. 01
    Call before you come. Confirm the car is on the floor and that someone can open it for you. Stock moves week to week; {{PUBLISHED}} cars are published here out of roughly {{FLOOR}} on the books.
  2. 02
    Walk around it cold. Ask for the engine to be off when you arrive. A cold start tells you more than ten minutes of driving.
  3. 03
    Look at the papers, not just the paint. RC, insurance expiry, PUC, service invoices. If a document is missing, that is worth knowing before a deposit is discussed.
  4. 04
    Drive it on your roads. A two-lap loop proves nothing. Ask for the run to include a stretch you actually use — Bypass traffic, a flyover, a monsoon puddle if the season allows.
  5. 05
    Then decide at the counter. Price, exchange of your car, finance introductions and RC transfer are all settled in the same place, with the same person.
