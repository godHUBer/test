Privacy, terms, and where

Three things a car website usually buries, written out because they are short.

Privacy

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Its fonts, photographs and script are served from the same place as the pages, so your browser asks nothing of a third party while you browse — no ad network learns that you looked at a Creta.

The enquiry and sale forms do not transmit data to a server. They compose an email on your own device: the fields are written out into a message in your mail app, and nothing leaves the device unless you press send. No account, no storage, no tracking pixel, no newsletter signup.

If you email or telephone the showroom, the dealership’s ordinary business records apply — your contact details, the car in question and the content of the conversation. Ask for those records to be removed by writing to {{EMAIL}}; a photograph you send for a valuation sits in their inbox until they delete it, so say if you want it gone.

About our listing data

Every specification, photograph and price on this site is taken from the dealership’s own published material: its website at {{SOURCE_SITE}} for the company details, and its dealer-directory listings at {{SOURCE_INVENTORY}} for the individual cars, captured on {{VERIFIED}}.

Where a published listing stated nothing — a transmission type, most often — the page records not stated in the listing. Fields are never inferred from the model name, because a variant that exists in a brochure need not be the car on the floor. The completeness line on each vehicle page tells you how many of the seven standard fields that car’s listing actually carried.

The dealership reports roughly {{FLOOR}} cars on its books, of which {{PUBLISHED}} are published with photographs online; the remainder are sold directly from the forecourt. That gap is stated on the collection page rather than hidden behind a “view all” button that leads nowhere.

Corrections

If you are the dealership and a figure here is stale, or you are a buyer and a car was not as described, write to {{EMAIL}} with the listing reference (each vehicle page prints one). Listing data is regenerated from a single file, so a correction lands everywhere at once — on the card, the filters and the vehicle page.
