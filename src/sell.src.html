Selling or exchanging

Exchange is a published service of this dealership, so if a car on the floor is your next one, the two figures can be settled against each other instead of you going out and back with cash.

Why we ask for this

Eight fields, because a valuation needs a car.

An instant online figure is arithmetic on assumptions: the model, the year and an average odometer. It costs nothing to produce and it is wrong often enough that sellers stop believing dealers. These fields exist so the first conversation is about your car — its history, its finish, what has been replaced — and so the inspection is worth both parties’ time.
