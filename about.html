The dealership

Sohan Automobile was established in 1997 under Mr. Sovan Dey and describes itself as West Bengal’s integrated multi-brand auto solution network. In practice that means one address in Beleghata where you can buy a car, hand over your old one, get the serviced, and sort the finance and insurance without being sent to three different offices.

The company’s own words for its philosophy are the plainest ones available: transparency, delivery on time, and getting it right the first time. That is also the standard this website is held to — a listing either states a fact or it says the fact has not been published.

Because the floor is multi-brand, the collection follows what is genuinely well kept rather than what any single manufacturer wants moved. You will find a 2025 XL6 beside a 2011 Estilo here, and both are presented the same way: year, variant, odometer, fuel, and asking price, with the photographs taken in the place the car actually sits.

How we work

Sourcing, pricing and
paperwork.

The three places where a used-car buyer usually gets an unsatisfactory answer, written down as this dealership handles them.

01 · Sourcing

One car at a time

Cars are bought in individually by the people who resell them, including from owners trading up. A car that is hard to explain tends not to come in — which is the most useful thing about a small floor.

02 · Pricing

One figure, discussed

The published price is the asking price, set per car rather than by a valuation engine. Where a listing records a reduction — the Vit’Brezza, for instance — the page says so instead of quietly changing the number.

03 · Paperwork

Transfer handled in-house

RC transfer, insurance and the finance introductions come from the same desk that sold the car. The dealership lists car finance and insurance among its services; lenders, rates and approval are set by the financier.

What this site publishes

Deliberately incomplete,
rather than invented.

Used-car websites are usually built the other way round: everything gets an answer because a template demands one. We have done the opposite. If the listing did not state the transmission, the page says not stated. If no warranty programme is published, the site does not mention a warranty.

  • Specifications copied from the dealership’s published listings, dated 24 November 2026.
  • Photographs taken by the dealership for those listings.
  • No testimonials, star ratings or awards, because none are published by the dealership.
  • No inspection certificate, warranty or buy-back promise is claimed anywhere on this site.
  • Opening hours, social channels and finance terms appear as labelled gaps until the dealership supplies them.
